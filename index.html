Islamabad,The Beautiful City

Islamabad, the capital city of Pakistan, is renowned for its serene environment, lush greenery, and meticulous urban planning.
Nestled against the scenic backdrop of the Margalla Hills in the northern Pothohar Plateau, the city was built during the 1960s to replace Karachi as the national capital.
Designed by the Greek architect Constantinos Apostolou Doxiadis, Islamabad is organized into distinct sectors and zones featuring wide, tree-lined avenues, spacious parks, and modern infrastructure. As the political and administrative hub of the country, it houses key government buildings, foreign embassies, and prestigious educational institutions. Beyond its official significance, Islamabad attracts visitors and residents alike with iconic landmarks such as the breathtaking Faisal Mosque, the Pakistan Monument, Daman-e-Koh, and Rawal Lake, offering a harmonious blend of natural beauty, cultural heritage, and peaceful city living.
